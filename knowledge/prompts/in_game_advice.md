# Prompt: in-game advice (fast)

For a quick question mid-game. Keep the answer short; you're alt-tabbed.

```
Read knowledge/00_dota_brain.md and knowledge/heroes/[hero].md.

Minute [mm]. I'm [hero], items: [items]. Net worth lead/deficit: [+/- gold].
Enemy cores and their key items: [hero: items, ...]
Situation: [e.g. "we lost mid T1, they have Aegis, I keep dying to Lion + PA"]

Answer in max 5 lines:
1) What to do in the next 2 minutes
2) Next item (and why, 1 line)
3) What to avoid
```
