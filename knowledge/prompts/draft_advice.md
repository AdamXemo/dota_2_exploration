# Prompt: draft advice

Copy, fill the brackets, paste into Claude (in this repo, so it can read the knowledge pack).

```
Read knowledge/00_dota_brain.md and knowledge/meta_snapshot.md.

I'm drafting as pos 1 in Ancient–Divine.
My team so far:  [heroes]
Enemy team:      [heroes]
Still unknown:   [n enemy picks]

From my pool (Anti-Mage, Morphling, Phantom Lancer, Wraith King, Templar Assassin,
Shadow Fiend, Arc Warden, Phantom Assassin):
1. Rank the top 3 picks for this draft. For each: one line on WHY (matchups, lane, timing).
2. For your #1 pick: starting items and the first 3 core items, adjusted for this enemy draft
   (read knowledge/heroes/<hero>.md).
3. The one enemy hero / item I must respect most, and how.
Keep it under 15 lines.
```

The app's **Draft** page does step 1 with numbers (base winrate + matchups); use this
prompt when you also want the reasoning.
