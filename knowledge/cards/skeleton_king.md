# Wraith King — card (7.41, Ancient–Divine)
Tier S · WR 53.4% · median GPM 578 · LH/min target 6.7
## Plan
- Win: Radiance + Blink + BKB; dive backline on two lives; take towers when Reincarnation is up
- Spike: Radiance ~16, Blink Dagger ~20, Aghanim's Shard ~22, Black King Bar ~25
## Build
Start: Tango, Quelling Blade, Gauntlets of Strength, Circlet, Iron Branch x2 | Lane: Phase Boots, Bracer (Magic Wand vs spell spam) | Core (min): Radiance ~16 → Blink Dagger ~20 → Aghanim's Shard ~22 → Black King Bar ~25 → Assault Cuirass ~32
## Pivot items
- vs key spellcaster or escape hero → Orchid Malevolence
- vs evasive or slippery core, or 2nd lockdown → Abyssal Blade
- vs Ghost Scepter / Glimmer saves → Nullifier
- tempo game, fight at 14-15 min → Armlet (instead of Radiance)
- tower-pushing with skeletons → Desolator
- late "never dead" → Aghanim's Scepter (shorter Reincarnation)
## Danger
- Warlock -4.7pp: Upheaval, golems body-block
- Keeper of the Light -4.6pp: blind, knockback
- Phantom Lancer -4.6pp (raw -7.7): illusion swarm (+ Diffusal mana burn)
- Anti-Mage -4.4pp: Mana Break before level 18
## Rules
- Fight only with Reincarnation up; disengage when it is on cooldown
- Keep mana for Reincarnation (220/110/0 mana); Wraithfire only with spare mana
- Blink in after key stuns are spent, not before
