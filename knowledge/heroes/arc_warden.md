# Arc Warden (Ranged, AGI, pos 1/2)

> Two heroes in one: Tempest Double copies Arc and his items. Double Mjollnir, double Hex, double Bloodthorn, a push monster that ends games from far away.

**Patch 7.41, Ancient+Divine:** 51.97% winrate (adjusted 51.85%, tier A) over 46,244 games; picked in 4.9% of matches.

**Win condition:** Midas + Maelstrom/Mjollnir into split-push with the Double, then fight with doubled actives (Orchid/Bloodthorn, Hex, Gleipnir) under Magnetic Field.

## Why it works
- Tempest Double duplicates Arc with all items: every active item is effectively bought twice.
- The innate Runic Infusion gives permanent stats from every Power Rune Arc or the Double activates. Power rune control = free stats.
- Magnetic Field: attack speed + evasion vs attacks from outside the field. Great for sieging towers.
- Spark Wraith: long-range magic damage + slow, used for zone control, farming and harass.
- Flux: a slow plus damage on isolated targets; it silences with Aghanim's.

## Power spikes
- Midas (~10 min)
- Maelstrom (~14 min)
- Mjollnir + Boots of Travel (~21 min)
- Bloodthorn / Hex (~28 min)

## Skills
Flux early for lane kills, Magnetic Field for push/defense, Spark Wraith for farm/harass; Tempest Double at 6/12/18.

- **Aghanim's Scepter:** Flux silences isolated targets, deals more damage, lasts longer.
- **Aghanim's Shard:** The Tempest Double spawns with Arcane + Invisibility + Haste rune buffs for ~12s (no Runic Infusion).
- **Verdict:** Shard is strong for pickoffs. Scepter is situational vs a single slippery core.

- **Hand of Midas** — Two Midases (you + Double): huge gold and XP.

## Core build (Ancient–Divine target timings)
- ~14 min **Maelstrom** — Chain lightning farms waves and camps.
- ~19 min **Boots of Travel** — Double teleports to other lanes: split push everywhere.
- ~22 min **Mjollnir** — Massive attack speed + bounces on both bodies.
- ~28 min **Bloodthorn** — Two silences that each amplify damage.
- ~32 min **Scythe of Vyse** — Scythe of Vyse: two Hexes = 7s of lockdown.

## Situational
- **Hurricane Pike** — Vs divers.
- **Black King Bar** — Vs heavy disable lineups.
- **Gleipnir** — Gleipnir: AoE roots twice, good vs slippery heroes.
- **Blink Dagger** — Catch-from-fog setups.
- **Witch Blade** — Alternative right-click line into Parasma.

## Struggles vs
- **Strong early pressure / tempo** — Midas build is slow; dying early ruins the scaling curve.
- **Divers (Storm, Slark, Spirit Breaker)** — Low HP, no escape beyond Field.
- **AoE that kills the Double** — Double dies = gold bounty and lost DPS.
- **Broodmother, Meepo, Lycan** — Worst matchups (−10.7 / −9.1 pp) (Research 04, Ancient–Divine data): Flux deals no damage when another enemy unit is near the target.

## Thrives vs
- **Slow, farm-oriented drafts** — Arc out-farms and out-pushes them.
- **Teams with no wave clear** — Split-push wins the game without fighting.

## Tips
- Lane: Use the Double for Midas charges and rune control.
- Lane: Spark Wraith on rune spots and lane entrances.
- Mid: Double pushes the opposite lane while you farm.
- Mid: Every Power Rune is +stats via Runic Infusion: take them.
- Late: Use the doubled items in sequence: Hex, Hex, Bloodthorn, Bloodthorn.
- Late: Field on top of your team at high ground.

## Common mistakes
- Micro tunnel vision: watching only the Double and losing the main hero.
- Skipping Midas and then being too weak to fight anyway.
