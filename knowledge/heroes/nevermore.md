# Shadow Fiend (Ranged, AGI, pos 2/1)

> Soul collector with three Shadowrazes and a giant AoE nuke ult. Snowballs from mid lane; scales with souls and right-click damage.

**Patch 7.41, Ancient+Divine:** 41.06% winrate (adjusted 41.46%, tier D) over 64,847 games; picked in 6.9% of matches.

**Win condition:** Max souls early, use Raze + right-click to take control, and fight with Requiem + BKB. NOTE: SF is the weakest hero of your pool this patch in Ancient-Divine (~41% WR), so play him only in safe drafts.

## Why it works
- The innate Necromastery gives damage per soul (4 souls per hero kill). Last hits = damage, so SF snowballs faster than other mids.
- Three Shadowrazes at different ranges: wave clear, last hits, harass and kill pressure. Each hit stacks a damage amplifier.
- Feast of Souls gives bonus move and attack speed and collects souls from nearby enemies: chase + DPS burst.
- Presence of the Dark Lord: an armor-reduction aura that boosts all physical damage on nearby enemies.
- Requiem of Souls: a massive AoE nuke and fear around SF. With Blink or BKB it decides teamfights.

## Power spikes
- Full souls + level 6
- Mask of Madness or Falcon (~10 min)
- Manta or BKB (~20 min)
- Daedalus (~26 min)

## Skills
Shadowraze max early (wave clear and kills), Necromastery souls passively, Feast of Souls for DPS; Requiem at 6/12/18.

- **Aghanim's Scepter:** Requiem has a lower cooldown and the wave returns to SF, healing him for the damage dealt.
- **Aghanim's Shard:** Shadowraze cooldown -2s per hero hit and a stacking slow.
- **Verdict:** Shard is very strong for laning and fight pressure (~15+ min). Scepter is good vs grouped teams in long games.

## Tips
- Lane: Raze waves only when the enemy isn't nearby to deny you; secure souls.
- Lane: Use raze hits to set up the amplifier stack, then right-click.
- Mid: Farm camps with razes between waves.
- Mid: Fight with souls; dying loses 30% of them.
- Late: Requiem from fog or after Blink with BKB on.
- Late: Stand behind the frontline; Presence shreds armor in range.

## Common mistakes
- Picking SF into a heavy dive lineup.
- Raze-spamming waves without mana for kills.
- Fighting with no souls after a death.
