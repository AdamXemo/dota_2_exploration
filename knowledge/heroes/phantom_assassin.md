# Phantom Assassin (Melee, AGI, pos 1)

> Crit, blink and evade. Phantom Strikes onto a target, Coup de Grace crits delete it, Immaterial makes right-clickers miss.

**Patch 7.41, Ancient+Divine:** 50.02% winrate (adjusted 50.02%, tier B) over 82,935 games; picked in 8.8% of matches.

**Win condition:** Battle Fury or Desolator timing into BKB, then fight: Phantom Strike onto the weakest target, crit, move to the next.

## Why it works
- Coup de Grace: a chance to gain Deadly Focus, guaranteeing a big crit. Burst damage scales with every point of attack damage.
- Phantom Strike: blink to any unit plus attack speed when targeting an enemy. Gap closer and escape (blink to a creep/ally).
- Immaterial gives evasion that grows with level. Physical carries miss many attacks unless they have MKB/Bloodthorn.
- The innate Blur makes PA invisible on the minimap far from enemies and lets her dodge projectiles on cast. Great for surprise.
- Stifling Dagger: ranged slow + damage that applies attack effects and can crit. Last-hit and harass tool.

## Power spikes
- Desolator (~14 min) or Battle Fury (~15 min)
- BKB (~21 min)
- Basher / Abyssal (~27 min)
- Satanic (~32 min)

## Skills
Stifling Dagger early for last hits and harass, Phantom Strike for kills/escape, Immaterial as needed; Coup de Grace at 6/12/18.

- **Aghanim's Scepter:** Fan of Knives: AoE damage based on max HP + Break.
- **Aghanim's Shard:** Blur lower cooldown; hero kills advance your cooldowns by 60%.
- **Verdict:** Scepter's Break is strong vs WK, Spectre, Bristleback. Shard is a solid snowball pickup.

## Core build (Ancient–Divine target timings)
- ~15 min **Battle Fury** — Farm speed; cleave crits clear camps. (Or Desolator first for tempo.)
- ~19 min **Desolator** — -armor: crits become lethal.
- ~23 min **Black King Bar** — PA dies to disables; BKB keeps you hitting.
- ~29 min **Abyssal Blade** — Stun through BKB + bash.
- ~33 min **Satanic** — Strength + lifesteal: crits heal you a lot.

## Situational
- **Monkey King Bar** — Vs evasion (enemy PA, Butterfly).
- **Nullifier** — Vs Ghost Scepter / Glimmer: common answers to PA.
- **Aghanim's Scepter** — Fan of Knives Break vs passive heroes.
- **Sange and Yasha** — Vs slows; cheaper fight item.
- **Orb of Corrosion** — Early vs healing lanes.

## Struggles vs
- **MKB / Bloodthorn / true strike** — Ignores Immaterial.
- **Ghost Scepter, Glimmer, ethereal saves** — Physical-only damage.
- **Armor stackers (Dragon Knight, Assault)** — Crit damage falls off.
- **Heavy disable lineups pre-BKB** — Low HP melee hero.
- **Bristleback, Troll Warlord, Axe, Omniknight** — Data-backed counters (Research 04, Ancient–Divine data).

## Thrives vs
- **Right-click carries without MKB** — Immaterial makes them miss.
- **Squishy supports** — Strike + crit = instant kill.

## Tips
- Lane: Dagger for ranged last hits and to deny.
- Lane: Strike to a creep in the enemy wave to escape or reposition.
- Mid: Blur in the fog, then Strike onto a support.
- Mid: Battle Fury build: farm until the BKB timing, don't over-fight.
- Late: Strike onto the backline once key disables are used.
- Late: Watch for MKB on the enemy carry and stop relying on evasion.

## Common mistakes
- Diving without BKB into a team with stuns.
- Ignoring the enemy's MKB / Ghost Scepter and still building only crit.
