# Templar Assassin (Ranged, AGI, pos 2/1)

> Huge physical burst from invisibility. Refraction blocks damage and amplifies hits, Meld deletes armor, and Psionic Traps give map control and slows. Strongest as an early-mid tempo carry.

**Patch 7.41, Ancient+Divine:** 46.25% winrate (adjusted 46.50%, tier D) over 42,319 games; picked in 4.5% of matches.

**Win condition:** Desolator + Blink timing to snowball kills and take towers before 25 min; then convert into BKB/Daedalus and end before the hard carries scale.

## Why it works
- Refraction blocks a fixed number of damage instances (any size), so it is great vs many small hits and nukes. It also gives bonus damage on several hits.
- Meld: standing still turns you invisible; the next attack deals bonus damage and reduces armor. Meld + Desolator lowers armor massively.
- Psi Blades split attacks to units behind the target: last-hit through creeps and harass behind them.
- Psionic Traps give vision and slows anywhere; Psionic Projection teleports to a trap for flank kills.
- The innate Inner Peace gives regen when you stand still and untouched, so you can stay in lane.

## Power spikes
- Level 2-3 Refraction + Meld trades
- Desolator (~14 min)
- Blink (~18 min)
- BKB + Daedalus (~26 min)

## Skills
Refraction and Meld for burst and trades; Psi Blades value points; Psionic Trap at 6/12/18. Refraction charges matter more than Meld damage early.

- **Aghanim's Scepter:** Psionic Traps silence enemies when triggered.
- **Aghanim's Shard:** Refraction gets more bonus damage and can be cast while disabled.
- **Verdict:** Shard is often good mid game (escape stuns into Refraction). Scepter is a situational pickoff tool.

## Starting items
- **Magic Wand** — Most common mid start: stats + charges from constant spell casts in mid.
- **Faerie Fire** — Two Faerie Fires: last-hit damage for the mid duel plus two burst heals.
- **Faerie Fire** — Second one. Ask a support for a Tango.

### Starting variants
- *Mid lane, stat start*: Tango, Faerie Fire, Iron Branch, Iron Branch, Circlet, Slippers of Agility. 560g: more stats now, Wraith Band later. Blades of Attack comes later on the way to Falcon Blade.
- *Safe lane carry*: Tango, Quelling Blade, Iron Branch, Iron Branch, Slippers of Agility, Circlet. Play for Desolator + Dragon Lance farm, slower timings.

## Laning items
- **Power Treads** — Attack speed.
- **Falcon Blade** — HP + damage + mana regen for Refraction spam.
- **Dragon Lance** — Range to farm and harass safely.
- **Wraith Band** — Cheap stats.

## Core build (Ancient–Divine target timings)
- ~14 min **Desolator** — Stacks with Meld's armor reduction: delete supports and towers.
- ~18 min **Blink Dagger** — Initiate onto a target from Meld range.
- ~24 min **Black King Bar** — TA has low HP: survive the counter-stun.
- ~28 min **Daedalus** — Daedalus multiplies the already huge Meld hit.
- ~32 min **Hurricane Pike** — Range, stats, and the disengage you need vs divers.

## Situational
- **Monkey King Bar** — Vs evasion (Butterfly, PA, Windranger).
- **Swift Blink** — Late upgrade of Blink.
- **Orchid Malevolence** — Vs slippery mids (Storm, Puck, Void Spirit).
- **Aghanim's Shard** — Vs stun-heavy teams: Refraction while stunned.
- **Linken's Sphere** — Vs single-target lockdown (Doom, Lion Hex).

## Struggles vs
- **Many small damage instances (Gyro, Razor, Viper, Jakiro)** — Each tick/hit burns one Refraction charge.
- **True sight / dust + AoE** — Meld is countered by detection; traps get cleared.
- **Late-game hard carries** — TA falls off if she doesn't end.
- **Armor stackers** — Physical burst falls off.

## Thrives vs
- **Big single-nuke heroes (Lina, Lion, Zeus ult)** — One Refraction charge blocks a huge nuke.
- **Melee mids** — Psi Blades harass from behind creeps.

## Tips
- Lane: Psi Blade harass through the creep wave.
- Lane: Stand still for Inner Peace regen when you're safe.
- Mid: Place traps on rune spots and jungle entrances: free vision and kills.
- Mid: Meld on the enemy's walking path, then hit.
- Late: Don't fight long: burst, then reposition.
- Late: Fight around BKB; you have very little HP.

## Common mistakes
- Farming the jungle instead of using the Desolator timing to end.
- Walking into Gyro/Razor and losing all Refraction charges instantly.
