# Wraith King (Melee, STR, pos 1)

> Two lives, a stun and lifesteal. The most forgiving carry in the pool and currently one of the strongest at Ancient-Divine.

**Patch 7.41, Ancient+Divine:** 53.41% winrate (adjusted 53.32%, tier S) over 112,229 games; picked in 11.9% of matches.

**Win condition:** Hit Radiance or Armlet + Blink + BKB, jump on the backline with two lives, and take towers when Reincarnation is off cooldown.

## Why it works
- Reincarnation = a second life. The enemy must kill you twice, and on revive nearby enemies are slowed while skeletons attack them.
- The innate Vampiric Spirit gives lifesteal: right-click sustain without items. When you would die, you become a fast Wraith for a short time and keep fighting.
- Wraithfire Blast is a reliable ranged stun with a slow: your catch tool.
- Bone Guard collects charges from last hits and kills, then spawns skeletons that fight, push and soak damage. They also lifesteal.
- Mortal Strike gives periodic bonus-damage crits, and the Shard adds pure 'curse' damage.
- Simple kit: few buttons, so you can focus on positioning and farm patterns.

## Power spikes
- Level 6 Reincarnation
- Armlet or Radiance (~15 min)
- Blink (~19 min)
- BKB + Aghanim's Shard (~25 min)

## Skills
Wraithfire Blast for kills/escape, Mortal Strike and Bone Guard for farm/push; Reincarnation at 6/12/18. Talent for stun duration is strong.

- **Aghanim's Scepter:** Reincarnation cooldown reduced.
- **Aghanim's Shard:** Mortal Strike hits curse the target for 75% of the attack damage again as PURE damage after 3s; lifesteal applies.
- **Verdict:** Shard is very strong DPS for the cost (~20-25 min). Scepter is good late to be 'never dead', not a rush.

## Starting items
- **Tango** — Sustain.
- **Quelling Blade** — Last hits.
- **Gauntlets of Strength** — Strength = HP + damage, Bracer path.
- **Iron Branch** — Stats, wand.
- **Circlet** — Bracer.

### Starting variants
- *Default safe lane*: Tango, Quelling Blade, Gauntlets of Strength, Circlet, Iron Branch, Iron Branch. Into Bracer + Phase.
- *Aggressive lane / ranged harass vs you*: Tango, Quelling Blade, Gauntlets of Strength, Iron Branch, Iron Branch, Enchanted Mango, Faerie Fire. Extra regen; Magic Stick if they spam spells.

## Laning items
- **Phase Boots** — Damage, armor, chase speed: WK has no gap closer.
- **Bracer** — Cheap HP + damage.
- **Magic Wand** — Vs spell-spam lanes.
- **Armlet of Mordiggian** — Huge strength + damage toggle: the fast fight-now build.

## Core build (Ancient–Divine target timings)
- ~16 min **Radiance** — Farm by walking + evasion; pushes with skeletons. (Alternative: Armlet first for faster fights.)
- ~20 min **Blink Dagger** — WK's missing gap closer: Blink -> Wraithfire on the backline.
- ~22 min **Aghanim's Shard** — Pure damage curse on Mortal Strike.
- ~25 min **Black King Bar** — Keeps you hitting through disables while Reincarnation protects your life.
- ~32 min **Assault Cuirass** — Armor for you and the team, minus armor for enemies, fast tower kills.

## Situational
- **Armlet of Mordiggian** — Instead of Radiance when you want to fight at 14-15 min (tempo games).
- **Desolator** — More burst; good with skeletons to take towers.
- **Abyssal Blade** — Vs evasive/slippery cores, or a second lockdown.
- **Orchid Malevolence** — Vs a key spellcaster or escape hero; Bloodthorn late.
- **Aghanim's Scepter** — Late, to shorten Reincarnation cooldown.
- **Nullifier** — Vs Ghost Scepter / Glimmer saves.

## Struggles vs
- **Mana burn (Anti-Mage, Nyx)** — No mana = no Reincarnation.
- **Break (Silver Edge, Doom, PA Fan of Knives)** — Disables passives, including lifesteal and your second life.
- **Heal reduction (Spirit Vessel, AA, Skadi)** — Cuts lifesteal sustain.
- **Kiting heroes (Drow, Razor, Viper)** — WK has no built-in gap closer before Blink.

## Thrives vs
- **Burst lineups** — They kill you once and then you come back.
- **Melee carries** — Stun + lifesteal + slow wins duels.

## Tips
- Lane: Use Bone Guard charges to push the wave when the lane gets hard.
- Lane: Wraithfire Blast + 2-3 hits wins trades from level 2-3.
- Mid: Fight when Reincarnation is ready; disengage when it is on cooldown.
- Mid: Skeletons push side lanes for free map pressure.
- Late: Always keep mana for Reincarnation (treads INT switch or extra mana).
- Late: Blink in after key stuns are used, not before.

## Common mistakes
- Getting into a fight with Reincarnation on cooldown.
- Spending mana on Wraithfire and having none left for the ult.
- Ignoring Silver Edge / Break on the enemy.
