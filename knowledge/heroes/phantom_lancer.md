# Phantom Lancer (Melee, AGI, pos 1)

> The illusion army. Every hit has a chance to spawn more PLs, so mid-to-late game the enemy fights 20 heroes and can't find the real one.

**Patch 7.41, Ancient+Divine:** 52.73% winrate (adjusted 52.65%, tier S) over 100,068 games; picked in 10.6% of matches.

**Win condition:** Get Manta + Aghanim's, flood the map, and fight when the enemy has no AoE to clear illusions. Real PL hides in the crowd.

## Why it works
- Juxtapose: attacks (including illusion attacks) can create new illusions. Illusion count snowballs with attack speed and agility.
- The innate Illusory Armaments gives fresh illusions a minimum damage for a few seconds, so even early illusions hurt.
- Doppelganger: dodges projectiles and disables and shuffles PL with his illusions, so the enemy loses track of the real one.
- Phantom Rush: a gap closer that triggers on attack order. Aghanim's adds range and spawns illusions while rushing through enemies.
- Illusions split-push, scout, and absorb spells and tower aggro.

## Power spikes
- Level 7 with Doppel + Lance + Rush
- Manta (~19 min)
- Aghanim's Scepter (~24 min): illusion engage
- Skadi/Heart (~30+ min)

## Skills
Spirit Lance for lane harass and last hits, Juxtapose for illusion scaling, Doppelganger and Rush as needed; ult-level breakpoints matter less, since Juxtapose is the core passive.

- **Aghanim's Scepter:** Phantom Rush gets more range and creates illusions when rushing through enemies and every 600 units rushed.
- **Aghanim's Shard:** Juxtapose active: invisible for up to 8s with a decoy and +15% move speed.
- **Verdict:** Scepter is CORE in this patch: it's on most PL builds right after Manta. Shard is a good escape/reposition tool later.

## Core build (Ancient–Divine target timings)
- ~18 min **Manta Style** — Agility + illusions that themselves spawn more illusions; dispel.
- ~24 min **Aghanim's Scepter** — Rush illusions: turns every engage into an instant army.
- ~30 min **Eye of Skadi** — Stats and HP for the real PL; slows on hit.
- ~34 min **Heart of Tarrasque** — Survival: real PL needs HP once found.
- ~38 min **Butterfly** — Agility, evasion, illusion damage.

## Situational
- **Diffusal Blade** — Vs mana-dependent heroes: illusion mana burn drains whole teams.
- **Orchid Malevolence** — Vs a single slippery or key spellcaster; Bloodthorn late.
- **Black King Bar** — Vs heavy lockdown on the real hero (once they learn to find you).
- **Radiance** — Old-school burn build, situational vs illusion/summon lineups.
- **Aghanim's Shard** — As an escape/reposition tool late.

## Struggles vs
- **AoE illusion clearers (Ember, Earthshaker, Leshrac, Sven, Medusa)** — Illusions take extra damage and die to AoE in one go.
- **Mjollnir / Battle Fury carries** — Bounce and cleave kill illusions passively.
- **Tidehunter, Underlord, Dark Seer, Axe** — Top data counters (−5 to −6 pp) (Research 04, Ancient–Divine data): AoE + illusions take 600% damage.

## Thrives vs
- **Single-target lineups** — They can't kill 15 targets.
- **Anti-Mage, mana-reliant heroes** — Illusions with Diffusal drain mana; AM can't find the real PL.

## Tips
- Lane: Lance for last hits and harass; the illusion it spawns also hits.
- Lane: Doppelganger dodges projectile stuns (e.g. Storm Hammer).
- Mid: Send illusions to push other lanes and farm in the jungle.
- Mid: Doppel then walk the real PL away from the obvious spot.
- Late: The real PL should never be the first one hit. Let illusions engage.
- Late: Use the Shard to reposition invisibly before fights.

## Common mistakes
- Fighting into Ember/ES with no BKB.
- Always moving the real PL in the front of the pack (easy to spot).
