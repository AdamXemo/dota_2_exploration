# Anti-Mage (Melee, AGI, pos 1)

> The purest farmer in Dota. Blinks across the map, clears with Battle Fury + Manta illusions, and wins the game by out-scaling everyone after 30 minutes.

**Patch 7.41, Ancient+Divine:** 50.88% winrate (adjusted 50.87%, tier B) over 162,199 games; picked in 17.1% of matches.

**Win condition:** Hit ~30 min with Battle Fury, Manta and a third big item while the team survives; then split-push the map so the enemy must answer you, and join fights only when Mana Void can end one.

## Why it works
- Blink has a very short cooldown, so AM can farm lanes AND jungle camps all over the map faster than anyone else. Map coverage = gold.
- Battle Fury's cleave + Manta's illusions multiply clear speed: every wave and camp dies to a few hits.
- Mana Break burns mana per hit, and the innate Persecutor slows targets that are low on mana. Int heroes and anyone who spams spells end up out of mana and unable to run.
- Counterspell gives passive magic resistance and reflects targeted spells, so stunning AM with a targeted spell is risky.
- Mana Void punishes the enemy team for being low on mana. After AM has been hitting someone, the burst is huge in an area.

## Power spikes
- Level 6 (Mana Void kill threat in lane)
- Battle Fury (~14 min): farm speed doubles
- Manta (~20 min): can now take fights vs 1-2 heroes
- 3rd item (~28-30 min): real teamfight carry

## Skills
Mana Break + Blink early (Blink gets maxed for farm/escape); one value point in Counterspell for magic resistance; Mana Void at 6/12/18. Take the farm/survival talents.

- **Aghanim's Scepter:** After Blink, the next Mana Break burns an extra 20% max mana and blocks mana regen. A catch tool for very late games.
- **Aghanim's Shard:** Reflecting a spell with Counterspell also spawns a blinked fragment of AM next to the caster.
- **Verdict:** Both are late luxury buys. Shard is fine ~25+ min vs heavy targeted-spell lineups.

## Starting items
- **Tango** — Lane sustain.
- **Quelling Blade** — Last hits are everything, and it folds into Battle Fury.
- **Iron Branch** — Cheap stats; later Magic Wand.
- **Circlet** — Into Wraith Band if the lane is hard.
- **Slippers of Agility** — Agility for last-hit damage and armor; Wraith Band component.

### Starting variants
- *Easy lane (passive offlaner)*: Tango, Quelling Blade, Iron Branch, Iron Branch, Slippers of Agility, Circlet. Rush Ring of Health; skip Wraith Band.
- *Hard lane (aggressive offlaner / dual lane)*: Tango, Quelling Blade, Iron Branch, Iron Branch, Circlet, Faerie Fire, Enchanted Mango. More regen; build Magic Wand + Wraith Band before Ring of Health.

## Laning items
- **Power Treads** — Attack speed for farm; switch to INT before Blink/Void to save mana.
- **Ring of Health** — Regen to stay in lane, and the first Battle Fury piece.
- **Wraith Band** — Only if the lane is contested.
- **Magic Wand** — Vs spell-spam lanes.

## Core build (Ancient–Divine target timings)
- ~15 min **Battle Fury** — Farm accelerator: cleave, regen, chop trees for juke paths.
- ~21 min **Manta Style** — Stats, illusions that farm and push, and a basic dispel for silences and roots.
- ~29 min **Abyssal Blade** — Lockdown that pierces BKB; stops the targets you can't otherwise catch.
- ~31 min **Eye of Skadi** — Huge stats (HP!) plus a slow; AM is fragile without it.
- ~36 min **Butterfly** — Agility, damage and evasion vs physical lineups.

## Situational
- **Black King Bar** — Vs chain-stun lineups (Lion + Shaman + Bane...). Counterspell does not stop AoE disables.
- **Linken's Sphere** — Vs single-target lockdown ultimates: Doom, Legion Duel, Batrider Lasso, Lion/Shaman Hex.
- **Orb of Corrosion** — Early, vs healing lanes or when you need to win trades vs a melee offlaner.
- **Nullifier** — Vs Ghost Scepter / Glimmer / Eul's saves on the enemy carry or supports.
- **Monkey King Bar** — Vs PA, Butterfly carries, Windranger.
- **Swift Blink** — Last slot luxury.

## Struggles vs
- **Axe** — Berserker's Call goes through Blink escapes; Counter Helix shreds AM's low HP early.
- **Legion Commander** — Duel is a single-target lock; needs Linken's or Counterspell timing.
- **Phantom Lancer** — Mana Break on illusions is weak, and PL out-farms and out-fights AM in the mid game.
- **Fast tempo lineups** — If enemies take towers early, AM's long timings never arrive.
- **Heavy lockdown chains** — Bane, Shadow Shaman, Lion: a disable chain before AM reacts.

## Thrives vs
- **Int cores (Storm, Medusa, Zeus, Leshrac)** — Mana Break plus Persecutor empties and slows them; Mana Void finishes.
- **Slow, passive lineups** — Every minute of peace = a free item.

## Tips
- Lane: Last-hit > everything. Don't trade unless the enemy is out of mana.
- Lane: Blink early only for escape or to reach a stacked camp.
- Lane: Pull aggro to tank creeps if the offlaner zones you.
- Mid: After Battle Fury, cycle lane -> camps -> next lane. Never stand still.
- Mid: Join fights only if a Mana Void kill or a free objective is available.
- Mid: Split-push the side lane opposite the enemy carry when your team is grouped.
- Late: Keep Blink for escape, not engage, unless BKB is up.
- Late: Void the target with the lowest mana% in a cluster, not the lowest HP.

## Common mistakes
- Farming the jungle while your team loses towers 4v5 for 10 minutes.
- Using Blink to chase and having no escape when the counter-gank comes.
- Building BKB by default when the enemy has no real disables.
