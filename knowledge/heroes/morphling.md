# Morphling (Ranged, AGI, pos 1/2)

> Shape-shifting water carry. Swaps Strength and Agility on the fly: tanky when focused, a glass cannon when bursting. Highest skill ceiling of your mains.

**Patch 7.41, Ancient+Divine:** 47.90% winrate (adjusted 48.05%, tier C) over 39,356 games; picked in 4.2% of matches.

**Win condition:** Reach Manta + a lifesteal or Satanic and a Butterfly/Skadi item, then fight with Waveform engages, agility burst and Attribute Shift as your 'second HP bar'.

## Why it works
- Attribute Shift turns Agility into Strength and back. Shifting to STR in a fight is like drinking a huge salve, so focus-firing Morph is often a trap.
- The innate Ebb and Flow gives cast range and slow resistance from STR, and attack range and move speed from AGI. Your stat balance changes your whole hero.
- Adaptive Strike stuns (if STR is high) or deals heavy AGI-scaled damage (if AGI is much higher than STR). Pick the mode with your shift.
- Waveform makes you invulnerable while it travels: a dodge, an escape and an engage in one.
- Morph copies an enemy's basic abilities. Morphing into a strong-spell hero (Tiny, Lion, Zeus...) gives extra burst or utility.

## Power spikes
- Level 6-7 with Waveform + Adaptive Strike lane kills
- Manta (~20 min)
- Lifesteal/Satanic: you can now shift-sustain through fights
- Butterfly/Skadi (~30 min)

## Skills
Waveform early for farm + escape, Attribute Shift is always available (it's a toggle), Adaptive Strike to max later or points as needed; Morph at 6/12/18. Shift to STR when low, AGI when farming/bursting.

- **Aghanim's Scepter:** Morph alt-cast creates a strong illusion of the target that can cast its basic abilities; you can swap into it.
- **Aghanim's Shard:** Attribute Shift works while stunned and costs less mana.
- **Verdict:** Shard is great vs stun lineups (you can still shift to STR while stunned). Scepter is a late, skill-heavy luxury.

## Core build (Ancient–Divine target timings)
- ~19 min **Manta Style** — Agility, dispel, illusions for farm. Your first big item.
- ~22 min **Dragon Lance** — Attack range so you can shoot from safety (Ebb and Flow adds more).
- ~29 min **Butterfly** — Agility, evasion, attack speed: massive DPS + survivability.
- ~31 min **Black King Bar** — Stops the stun chains you can't shift through.
- ~35 min **Satanic** — Lifesteal plus strength: pairs with STR shift to never die.

## Situational
- **Ethereal Blade** — Burst build: E-Blade + Adaptive Strike one-shots supports and int cores.
- **Eye of Skadi** — Vs kiting/healing lineups; gives HP so you need fewer STR shifts.
- **Hydra's Breath** — Late game AoE right clicks (Morph is ranged).
- **Linken's Sphere** — Vs Doom/Hex/Duel-style single-target disables.
- **Aghanim's Shard** — Vs heavy stun teams.

## Struggles vs
- **Mana burn and silence (Anti-Mage, Nyx, Silencer, Orchid)** — Without mana you can't shift or Waveform; silence = no Attribute Shift.
- **Ancient Apparition** — Ice Blast blocks the heal from shifting to STR.
- **Physical burst with lockdown** — You don't get time to shift.

## Thrives vs
- **Magic-burst lineups** — Shift to STR + BKB and they run out of damage.
- **Teams with strong morph targets** — Morph turns their best spells against them.

## Tips
- Lane: Waveform through the wave = free last hits and a safe escape.
- Lane: Stay slightly AGI-heavy for last-hit damage, but shift to STR the moment you get dived.
- Mid: Pre-shift to STR before you walk into fog.
- Mid: Waveform + Adaptive Strike (AGI mode) kills isolated supports.
- Late: Fight from max range; shift is your HP bar, mana is your real HP: watch it.
- Late: Morph the enemy hero whose spells win fights (e.g. a stun or a big nuke).

## Common mistakes
- Running out of mana before the fight, so you can't shift.
- Shifting too late when already stunned (without Shard).
- Playing it like a normal right-click carry and ignoring Morph.
