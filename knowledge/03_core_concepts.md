# Core Dota concepts (beyond the carry)

Short explanations of the ideas that decide games, written so an LLM can quote them and a
learning player can act on them. Mechanics numbers: `01_mechanics.md`.

## 1. Lane equilibrium & creep aggro
- **Equilibrium** = where the two creep waves meet. You want it **near your tower**: safe for you,
  dangerous for the enemy who must walk up to last-hit.
- **Pushed lane?** Stop hitting creeps (only last hits), let it bounce back, or have a support
  **pull** a neutral camp (x:15/x:45) so your wave dies in the jungle and theirs walks to your tower.
- **Creep aggro:** right-clicking an enemy hero within ~500 range of their creeps makes those
  creeps target you for a couple of seconds. Right-click an enemy *hero* briefly near *your* creeps
  to pull the waves toward you (the "aggro pull"). Harass from outside their creeps' aggro.
- **Block the first wave** (walk in front of your creeps at 0:00) so equilibrium starts on your side.

## 2. Wave management (mid/late game)
- **Push the wave that is far from the enemy, then leave.** A pushed side lane forces an enemy
  to show on the map to defend, which is free information and space.
- **Never farm the lane closest to five missing enemies.** Count heroes on the minimap first.
- **Cut waves** (kill the enemy wave behind their tower line) to stop pressure on your base,
  only when the enemy is shown elsewhere.
- **Before a big fight or Roshan:** push all lanes out so creeps pressure the enemy while you fight.

## 3. Objectives (what actually wins)
| Objective | Why it matters |
|-----------|----------------|
| Tier 1–2 towers | Map control → safe farm for you, less for them; global gold. |
| Tormentor | Free Aghanim's Shard for a hero; cheap to take with 2–3 heroes mid game. |
| Roshan | Aegis = a carry who can't die once. Take it after a won fight, or when 3+ enemies are dead/shown far away. |
| High ground (T3 + barracks) | Barracks = mega creeps eventually. Only siege with Aegis, a big item lead, or the enemy carry dead. |
| Outposts | XP over time + teleport points. |

**Rule:** after every won fight ask "tower, Roshan, or Tormentor?" before farming.

## 4. Vision & information
- **Observer wards** = vision; **Sentry** = detection of invisible units and wards.
- As a carry you don't ward much, but you **buy Smoke/Dust when needed** and **read the map**:
  missing heroes = danger, especially at night.
- **Fog walking:** move in the fog (out of enemy vision) when rotating; never walk the same
  visible path twice.

## 5. Timings & power curves
- Every hero has a **power curve**: strong early (TA, PA-Desolator), strong mid (WK, PL), or
  strong late (AM, Spectre, Morph). Your draft's curve should match how you plan to win.
- **Fight on your spike, avoid fights just before it.** A carry 400 gold from BKB shouldn't
  take a fight that can wait 60 seconds.
- **Enemy spikes matter too:** "their Medusa just finished Skadi" means avoid open fights.

## 6. Teamfight basics (as a carry)
1. **Positioning:** stand where the enemy initiator can't reach you first.
2. **Wait for the key spells** (Black Hole, Ravage, Chronosphere) to be used or dodged before committing.
3. **Hit what you can hit safely**, not "the best target". Free DPS on the frontline > dying on the way to the support.
4. **BKB timing:** activate *before* the disable lands (when the initiator blinks), not after.
5. **After the fight:** objectives (see 3).

## 7. Economy concepts
- **Net worth lead** is what matters, not kills. A 0–5 carry with 3k more net worth is winning.
- **Buyback:** after ~30–35 min, keep buyback gold. A lost fight without buyback can end the game.
- **Comeback gold:** killing the enemy carry when you're behind gives a big bounty. One good
  pick-off can swing a game.
- **Stacking:** a support stacking 2–3 camps for you at 10–20 min = a free item.

## 8. Draft concepts
- **Lane matchups** (who wins the 0–10 min), **counters** (Research 04), **synergies** (e.g. PL +
  a support with save spells), **power curve** (see 5), **damage type balance** (don't draft 5 physical heroes into Ghost Scepter supports).
- **Last pick advantage:** carries are often picked late so they can counter. This is what the Draft Helper page is for.
